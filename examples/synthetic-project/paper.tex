We study a platform that jointly learns product quality, recommends sellers, and sets commissions when sellers can manipulate review signals.

Platforms increasingly combine pricing and recommendation decisions \citep{cao2026learning}. Reviews guide learning but can be strategically distorted \citep{luca2016fake}.
