# Seller Responses to Marketplace Disclosure

## Research question

How does a marketplace's disclosure of service-quality information change
seller investment, consumer demand, and platform welfare?

## Framework and primitives

The unit is a seller-week. Treatment is a staggered disclosure-policy rollout.
Outcomes include seller service investment, conversion, price, and retention;
heterogeneity is defined by pre-policy reputation and local competition.

## Central mechanism

Disclosure changes both consumer inference and sellers' return to quality
investment. The empirical design separates demand reallocation from supply-side
effort responses.

## Method

Staggered difference-in-differences with event-study diagnostics, seller and
time fixed effects, and robustness to treatment-effect heterogeneity.

## Key assumptions

Parallel untreated trends, no anticipatory seller response, and no cross-market
spillover during the main window.

## Contribution delta

The project measures the endogenous seller response that disclosure studies
often treat as fixed, and links it to platform-level welfare.

## Closest papers
