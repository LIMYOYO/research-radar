# Disclosure Design and Operational Trust

## Research question

Which disclosure format helps customers calibrate trust in an AI-assisted service decision?

## Framework and primitives

Participants choose whether to accept a service recommendation after seeing accuracy and process disclosures. Treatments vary uncertainty framing and human-oversight information.

## Central mechanism

Process transparency can improve calibration but also overload users; perceived control may mediate acceptance independently of objective accuracy.

## Method

Pre-registered randomized online experiment with comprehension checks, behavioral acceptance, confidence, and incentive-compatible performance outcomes.

## Key assumptions

The disclosure manipulation changes perceived transparency without changing the underlying recommendation; exclusions and outcomes are pre-specified.

## Contribution delta

The project connects disclosure design to operational adoption decisions and distinguishes trust level from trust calibration.
