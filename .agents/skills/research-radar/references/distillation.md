# Project-conditioned distillation

Use this standard only for candidates that survive the deterministic triage. The objective is to tell the researcher what changes in their project, not to paraphrase the abstract.

## Required fields

1. **Research question and setting** — the decision problem, actors, and environment.
2. **Framework** — primitives or constructs, timing, information, actions, and outcomes.
3. **Mechanism or identification** — the decisive logic, not a generic method label.
4. **Main result** — include boundary conditions only when supported by the evidence read.
5. **Project relationship** — `supports`, `contradicts`, `extends`, `competes`, `method-lead`, or `background`.
6. **Contribution delta** — what this paper has that the current project does not, and vice versa.
7. **Consequence** — what the researcher should reconsider in framing, assumptions, model, empirics, or citations.
8. **Action** — `read-now`, `cite`, `watch`, or ignore, with one sentence of justification.
9. **Evidence** — `full-text`, `abstract`, or `metadata`, plus the DOI/link and local PDF path when applicable.

## Analytical papers

Name players, sequence, information, payoff-relevant primitives, equilibrium or optimization concept, central comparative statics, and the assumption doing the most work. Compare mechanisms even when application keywords differ.

## Empirical papers

Name the unit and setting, data, treatment or variation, estimand, identification assumptions, main magnitude, and external-validity boundary. Separate descriptive facts from causal results.

## Experimental papers

Name assignment, treatments, outcome, manipulation checks, sample, and the behavioral mechanism. Do not translate a lab result into a field claim without qualification.

## Evidence discipline

Never invent details omitted from the available evidence. If only an abstract is available, explicitly list the questions that require full text. If a PDF is local, verify that the relevant passage exists before upgrading the evidence label. Distinguish the paper's claims from Codex's inference about how it affects the project.
