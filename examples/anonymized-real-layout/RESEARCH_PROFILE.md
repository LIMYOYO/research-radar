# Capacity Sharing Under Uncertain Demand

## Research question

When should two service providers share reserved capacity when demand is
uncertain and each provider privately observes a demand signal?

## Framework and primitives

Two providers choose capacity before observing demand and may trade unused
capacity after receiving private signals. A platform sets the trading rule.

## Central mechanism

Sharing pools idiosyncratic demand risk but weakens the incentive to reserve
capacity before demand is known.

## Method

Analytical model with equilibrium characterization and a small numerical
illustration.

## Key assumptions

Demand signals are affiliated, reservation cost is convex, and transferred
capacity is imperfectly substitutable for locally reserved capacity.

## Contribution delta

The project combines endogenous ex-ante reservation with ex-post capacity
trading under private demand information.

## Closest papers

The synthetic baseline paper studies capacity pooling without private signals.
The synthetic mechanism paper studies information disclosure without sharing.
