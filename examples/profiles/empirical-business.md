# Adoption Frictions in B2B Digital Marketplaces

## Research question

How does a marketplace's verification policy affect seller adoption, buyer trust, and transaction volume?

## Framework and primitives

The unit is a seller-week. The platform changes verification requirements across categories at different dates. Outcomes include adoption, conversion, price, and exit.

## Central mechanism

Verification can raise buyer trust while imposing a fixed compliance cost on sellers. The net effect should vary with prior reputation and category-level information asymmetry.

## Method

Staggered difference-in-differences with event-study diagnostics. The design uses policy timing and tests for anticipation, differential trends, and composition changes.

## Key assumptions

Parallel trends conditional on category and seller controls; no concurrent category-specific intervention; seller composition is measured and bounded.

## Contribution delta

The project separates the trust and compliance-cost channels and estimates heterogeneous adoption responses, rather than reporting only aggregate demand effects.
